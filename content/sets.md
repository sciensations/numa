## Source notes

The numbered student exercises stop before each “guide animateur”. Facilitator solutions, schedules, warm-ups, worksheet instructions, answer blanks, and the unnumbered S22 collectionneurs extension are not migrated. The separate history, strategy, special-session, and reserve worksheets are outside this batch.

FFJM/FSJM references follow the student DOCX and `suivi_problemes_utilises.xlsx`. The two “absent de l'index” references retain that note and have `tracker_row: none`:

- S09, “Des billes de toutes les couleurs” (0023), Quarts de finale 2019, problem 7.
- S33, “L'âge de Mathias” (0075), Quarts de finale 2011, problem 6.

The S14 tracker also lists “Le dessous des cartes (mise en route)”, index line 145. It is not one of the numbered student exercises. S14 instead starts with “L'échiquier tronqué”, attributed in the DOCX to Max Black (1946).

S28, “La carte à colorier” (0061), has an unresolved source discrepancy: the statement says six regions, but the drawing has seven. Both are preserved pending an editorial decision. Review this card before publication or printing.

Numa originals and classic puzzles retain their own attributions, with no invented competition or index numbers. Difficulty scores and topic tags are editorial browsing aids, separate from the source competition coefficient.
